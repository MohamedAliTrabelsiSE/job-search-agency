# Sample posting (fictional)

**Company:** Northwind Ledger
**Role:** Product Engineer
**Location:** Berlin, on-site
**Pay:** €70,000–€95,000

## The work

Build and look after HTTP APIs used by business customers. You will take a small feature from a conversation to production, including the failure cases.

## Requirements

- One product or substantial feature you built and can discuss.
- Production experience with TypeScript.
- You use coding agents and you review what they emit.

## What we do not require

No minimum number of years. No specific degree.

## Offer

Relocation support and visa support for someone who does not already have the right to work in Germany. Based in Berlin or willing to relocate.

## Not this role

We do not sponsor a role that says "existing right to work only". This sample is the opposite of that sentence, on purpose, so the gate has a posting it can pass.
