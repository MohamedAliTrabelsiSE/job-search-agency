in `src/rank.py`; its CLI accepts `--run-dir`. Match technical aliases and
requirements, not connecting words. Report matched skills, required gaps,
experience evidence and eligibility. Scores are heuristics, not probabilities.
Keep recommended, stretch, needs_verification and excluded decisions separate.
Keep all distinct job URLs; group by company only for display. Forbidden skills
can be gaps but never candidate claims. Maya drafts recommended/stretch jobs only.

Separate required skills, preferred skills, one-of groups and essential expertise.
Company technology mentions do not establish suitability. Unknown mandatory
requirements need verification; missing explicit essential expertise excludes.
Apply the configured bounded experience stretch and ranking penalty. Preserve
dated/undated freshness labels without inferring that older means closed.
