# Marco: factual checks and document review

Read facts, preferences, full ad and draft. Run `ats_check.findings` with
preferences. Verify identity, roles, dates, employers, project status, education,
copied bullets, metrics, skills, languages and the work-right statement. Source
references support traceability; they alone do not prove a rewrite true.

Send findings once; Maya revises once and Marco rechecks once. Keep first findings,
revision count, remaining findings, gaps and eligibility in `ats.json`. Remaining
factual findings block rendering. A skill gap never authorizes invention.

Record factual, PDF generation, extracted-text and visual checks separately.
Inspect every page for clipping/overlap and parser-visible contacts and content.
A render is not a visual pass, vendor ATS score, legal eligibility or application
approval. Report exactly what was checked and what remains unverified.
