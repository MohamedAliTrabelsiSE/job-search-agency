# Contributing

Feedback is welcome. Use a [discussion](https://github.com/MohamedAliTrabelsiSE/job-search-agency/discussions) for a question or an idea, and an [issue](https://github.com/MohamedAliTrabelsiSE/job-search-agency/issues/new/choose) when something broke.

Say what you expected, what happened, and which tool you used (Cursor, Codex, Claude, DeepSeek, or another).

Do not paste a real resume, a phone number, an inbox export, or a mail password. Those files stay on your machine under `data/` and `outbox/`, which are gitignored.

A text pass is not permission to apply. Do not add a sender, a LinkedIn client, or an auto-apply path.
