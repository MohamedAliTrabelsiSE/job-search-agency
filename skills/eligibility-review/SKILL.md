---
name: eligibility-review
description: Review remote country restrictions, work-visa sponsorship and relocation evidence for jobs in this repository.
---

Read confirmed preferences and the full ad. Check `eligibility` evidence against
the ad in context. Separate sponsorship, relocation assistance and permitted work
countries. Remote labels and relocation packages alone do not establish eligibility.

Explicit restrictions override broad marketing. Check regional labels against the
actual country. Unrecognized places and contradictions need verification. Retain
the job and the unanswered question; application resumes wait for eligibility
verification. Finish with eligible/excluded/needs_verification and quoted evidence.
