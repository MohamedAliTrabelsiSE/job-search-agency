---
name: resume-review
description: Verify factual provenance and rendered PDF quality for resumes generated by this repository.
---

Follow `prompts/ats.md`. Compare the draft with confirmed facts and the full ad;
run `ats_check.findings` with preferences. Keep every role/project/language.
Automated bullets match source claims; unverified rewrites are findings.

Give Maya findings once and recheck once. Render with the approved template and
renderer. Inspect every PDF page and extracted text before delivery. Report
factual, extraction, visual and job-fit outcomes separately. Keep private images
under ignored `tmp/`. Finish with artifact paths and scoped checks; a failed or
stale PDF is not an application file.
